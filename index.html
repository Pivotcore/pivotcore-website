Services

Supplier identification, sourcing, and purchasing support.

Supplier identification & vetting
Find capable, reliable suppliers that fit your production and quality requirements.

  • Risk assessment covering delivery performance, financial stability, and single‑source exposure

Strategic & tactical sourcing
Cost‑focused sourcing aligned with realistic SME volumes and constraints.
  • Should‑cost analysis and cost breakdown to understand true price drivers
  • Competitive sourcing exercises and RFQ structuring for clarity and comparability
  • Supplier consolidation where appropriate to reduce complexity and improve leverage
  • Negotiation planning and support, including walk‑away points and trade‑off options

Fractional procurement support for SMEs without in‑house purchasing teams.
  • Day‑to‑day purchasing support for key categories and suppliers
  • Basic purchasing process and data structure design (POs, approvals, supplier records)
  • Supplier performance tracking and simple KPI dashboards
  • Support with Incoterms, logistics options, and practical supply chain risk reduction
